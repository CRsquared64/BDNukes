# Third-party notes

BDNukes ships no third-party code or assets. At runtime it references, but does
not redistribute:
- BDArmory Continued (CC-BY-SA-2.0): its nuclear damage model and one small
  placeholder mesh (BDArmory/Parts/ArmorPlate/Panel), which is stripped at load.
- Harmony (MIT) and Module Manager, as separately installed dependencies.
All textures, meshes and sounds in BDNukes are generated procedurally in code.
